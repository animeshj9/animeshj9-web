# Galli Club: Hyderabad becomes a family ritual

Decision: 9 September 2026. Working brand, not trademark-cleared. This replaces the technical/commercial direction as the lab's featured experiment. FeedProof is retained as an earlier project; no MCP release-checker product was implemented.

## The buyer and the moment

A Hyderabad parent of a 3-7-year-old, often with a younger sibling, at home on a hot/rainy afternoon or between naps. They want something more memorable than another worksheet, without ordering supplies, researching a whole activity, or crossing town. The product sells a ready-to-play family moment grounded in the city the child calls home.

This fits the founder's real interests: Hyderabad, stories, history and parenting. It does not reuse employer information, work architecture, or private family details.

## The product is content you can use

- Free: **Piku's missing postcard**, a complete 10-20-minute adult-led adventure, with age 3-4 / 5-7 prompts, quiet/gentle-movement options, optional nickname, printable keepsake and four-page offline kit.
- Proposed **₹299 one-time Hyderabad at Home family pack**: three original 20-30-minute story-play sessions and a 12-page A4 PDF. Includes story scripts, activity instructions, printable game/drawing surfaces and a family passport. No-printer alternatives use whole sheets, crayons and fingertips.
- Three missions: **The four-colour post office** (Charminar), **The fort's secret signal** (Golconda), **A lake full of wishes** (Hussain Sagar).
- This is a finished first edition awaiting family playtesting, not an open preorder. Checkout is closed. No subscription, physical inventory, child account, ads, AI API call, or ongoing server cost is required by the product.

The full product lives outside `dist` in `products/galli-club/hyderabad-at-home.pdf`. It is not secretly a public file behind a pretend paywall. Free kit and actual-page preview live inside the public site's downloads folder. The repository remains private; when sales open, a digital-delivery provider can hold the full PDF.

## Five uncomfortable questions

1. **Why pay?** To avoid the work between “we need an activity” and a good shared experience. Three planned sessions at ₹299 implies roughly ₹100 per session, before printing if chosen. That is a pricing hypothesis; parents might reasonably prefer free ideas or physical kits.
2. **Why not ChatGPT or another kit?** Original character continuity, local identity, a ready-to-use script and carefully edited variants. These are editorial advantages, not a technical moat. Family playtests must establish whether the story holds attention. Don't sell generic AI-generated worksheets at a premium.
3. **Can one person operate it?** Yes at small scale: create and test a fixed PDF edition, deliver digitally, answer download questions. No shipping, weekly event inventory or child moderation. New stories still require real writing, illustration and testing; do not promise monthly delivery or passive income.
4. **Can we reach buyers without paid ads?** Potentially through opt-in apartment parent communities, school parent introductions and local parenting creators. The founder's city/family context creates plausible access, but no channel has been proven and no group has been contacted. Get admin permission for promotions and avoid exposing child photos or names.
5. **First ₹1, ₹1,000, ₹10,000?** First ₹299 sale proves someone paid; 4 sales = ₹1,196; 34 sales = ₹10,166. Ten paid families = ₹2,990. These are gross totals before payment fees, taxes, refunds, creation time and support—not forecasts. Use INR because this buyer is local. A US-dollar price would create unnecessary friction.

The weakest claim is willingness to pay for a PDF when free inspiration is abundant. Keep the first edition small. The free sample must make a parent say “that was easy and my child asked for more”; downloads alone are not validation.

## First 10 families and first money

1. Try the free sample with five consenting families: at least two with ages 3-4, two with ages 5-7, and one household with a younger sibling. No video or child data collection is needed. Watch or ask the grown-up where setup, wording, boredom or mess got in the way. Time estimates are currently untested.
2. Revise only observed problems. Specifically test whether the story is too wordy, the adult has to invent missing instructions, the quiet option helps, and the no-printer version is sufficient.
3. Reach the next five families through one permitted apartment-group post and personal introductions. Share the free link, not a payment demand. Ask parents whether they completed or happily adapted the story and whether they'd want another one next weekend.
4. Offer the full pack at the actual ₹299 price to parents who enjoyed the sample. After commercial setup, obtain real payments; don't count a friendly “sounds nice” as intent. A simple parent-facing payment page and private file delivery are enough.
5. Ask satisfied buyers to share the free adventure link with one other family. A later playdate/classroom license is an expansion hypothesis only, not a product promised by this edition.

Manual validation log fields: consenting parent alias, child age band only, source, sample tried, setup minutes, where interest changed, quiet/no-printer used, parent quote with permission, actual price offered, paid/refused and why. Don't record child names, photos, school identity or household addresses.

**Go/no-go after two weeks of active testing:** 10 households try a real session; at least 5 say they'd voluntarily do another; aim for 5 full-price purchases including 3 outside close friends/family. If parents like the free game but won't pay, test stronger content/packaging before creating more packs. If setup consistently exceeds five minutes or the parent must repair the story, fix that. If no-printer play is the preferred mode, invest in the adult cue-card experience, not more colouring sheets. Pause after 20 genuine offers with no sales rather than inventing a subscription.

## Quality, facts and safety boundaries

Original fiction, not folk history. Marked factual statements: Charminar has four minarets; Golconda is a fort in Hyderabad; Hussain Sagar is a lake between Hyderabad and Secunderabad. Sources:

- [Hyderabad District: Charminar](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourist-place/charminar/)
- [Hyderabad District: Golconda](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourist-place/golconda-fort/)
- [Hyderabad District: Hussain Sagar](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourist-place/hussain-sagar-lake/)

Do not import debatable legends or tourist superlatives from these pages into the story. The hero is an imaginary cardboard city, not an architectural illustration; it has five visible turrets and is never labelled as a precise Charminar image. Worksheets are functional blank activity surfaces, not historical diagrams.

Adult-led, seated-compatible activities; no required small loose pieces, water, climbing, food or loud noises. Quiet and age options are suggestions, not accessibility certifications or developmental promises. This edition is not professionally reviewed or child-tested yet. English + short Hinglish prompts; do not claim a full Hindi or Telugu translation.

## Build and later setup

Site route: `/galli-club/`. Local browser state only; no persistence or child-name transmission. One fetch loads the static story JSON. Keepsakes escape input and disable active content. All public assets are self-hosted.

`npm test` runs the adventure/variant/export tests alongside the lab regressions. `npm run check` checks local links, PDFs' presence, module syntax and DOM binding names. PDFs are rendered and visually reviewed separately; browser QA and real-child playtesting are still distinct launch tasks.

The existing GitHub Actions workflow covers this project automatically. Deploy and domain configuration remain off/deferred as requested; see [LAUNCH.md](LAUNCH.md). Do not enable FeedProof's paid CTA to sell Galli Club. For Galli Club later: choose a parent-facing INR checkout with digital delivery, confirm actual business/contact/refund/tax requirements, place the full pack in private delivery storage, and replace the explicitly closed sales button only after a real end-to-end purchase/download test. No new account or token is needed to review this build.

## Rebuilding the PDFs and artwork provenance

Run `python scripts/build-galli-pdfs.py` with ReportLab, Pillow, pypdf and DejaVu fonts installed. It rebuilds the 12-page full edition, the 4-page free kit and the 3-page preview extracted from the full edition. The preview page numbers intentionally retain their positions in the complete pack. Only checked-in outputs are deployed; Python isn't required by the live site.

Artwork: `dist/galli-club/assets/hyderabad-at-home.webp`, from one built-in imagegen illustration, converted to WebP for delivery without changing the composition. Prompt: “Landscape 1536x1024 original children's picture-book gouache/cut-paper illustration: two young Indian children around 4 and 6, friendly pigeon, thick crayons, large paper circles, homemade cardboard city inspired by Hyderabad, folded kite and cushion lake; vivid cobalt, marigold, raspberry and green; tactile matte paper; no screens, lettering, logos, scissors or tiny beads.” The initial request mentioned a four-tower-inspired landmark; the output is treated as imaginary because its turrets are not exact.
